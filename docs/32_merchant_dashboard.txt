# Merchant Dashboard (Phase 8, Step 2)

We built the central hub for seller operations.

## What we built:
The `views/merchant/dashboard.php` page.

## Why we built it:
Merchants have different needs than regular customers. They need to see their business stats, manage their stock, and fulfill orders. This dashboard organizes those specialized tools in one place.

## Key Technical Features:

1. **Access Control**:
   `requireRole("merchant")` prevents customers or unverified users from accessing seller tools.

2. **Action-Oriented UI**:
   Instead of just showing data, the dashboard focuses on "Tasks":
   - **List Product**: Quick link to the creation form.
   - **Inventory**: Link to the management table.
   - **Orders**: Link to the sales tracker.

3. **User Context**:
   The dashboard personalized the experience by pulling the Merchant's name from the `currentUser()` helper.
