# One-Click Saved Payment Logic (Phase 10, Final Polish)

We implemented a "Saved Card" feature that allows users to bypass the full payment form after their first successful transaction.

## What we built:
1.  **Database Table**: `payment_methods` to store masked card info (`last_four`, `expiry`).
2.  **Persistence Logic**: `process_simulated_payment.php` now captures card details on first use.
3.  **Conditional UI**: `checkout_payment.php` detects saved cards and offers a "One-Click" simplified view.

## Why we built it:
Friction is the enemy of conversion. By allowing a user to pay with a single click, we increase the likelihood of them completing their installments on time and making repeat purchases. This is the industry standard for BNPL apps.

## Key Technical Features:

1.  **Masked Storage**: 
    We never store the full card number (PCI Compliance simulation). We only store the `last_four` digits to identify the card to the user.
    
2.  **User Experience (UX)**:
    -   Dynamic button text in `calculate_installments.php`.
    -   "Use a different card" bypass logic for flexibility.
    -   Double-confirmation pop-ups for "One-Click" actions to prevent accidents.

3.  **Seamless Integration**:
    The system works identically for the initial downpayment and subsequent installment repayments.

## How to use:
Once a user has completed one payment, all future payment requests will default to their saved card. They simply click "Confirm" and the transaction is processed instantly.
