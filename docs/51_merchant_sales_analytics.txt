# Merchant Sales Analytics (Phase 11, Step 1)

We built a comprehensive financial dashboard for merchants to track their performance on the TQSEET platform.

## What we built:
The `views/merchant/sales.php` analytics page.

## Why we built it:
Merchants need transparency. By providing real-time data on sales volume and net revenue (after platform commissions), we build trust with our business partners.

## Key Technical Features:

1. **Aggregated SQL Queries**:
   We use `SUM()` and `COUNT()` in a single query to calculate the merchant's lifetime performance without loading thousands of individual records into memory.

2. **Cross-Table Visibility**:
   The dashboard joins `orders`, `products`, and `users` to provide the merchant with a full "Order Context"—knowing exactly who bought what.

3. **Dynamic Commission Simulation**:
   The script calculates a 5% platform fee on the fly. This ensures that the merchant is never surprised by payout amounts.

4. **Status-Driven UI**:
   The transaction list uses status-dependent color palettes (Green for Active, Red for Cancelled) to help merchants focus on successful sales.

## How to use:
Merchants access this page to monitor their daily sales and calculate their upcoming payouts from the TQSEET platform.
