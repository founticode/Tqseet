# Merchant Inventory Management (Phase 11, Step 2)

We transformed the basic product list into a high-performance inventory management tool for our merchants.

## What we built:
A redesigned `views/merchant/products.php` with a focus on usability and professional aesthetics.

## Why we built it:
As a merchant grows, they need a tool that makes it easy to scan dozens of products quickly. The new layout provides high-density information (Name, ID, Category, Price, Status) without feeling cluttered.

## Key Technical Features:

1. **State-Driven Styling**:
   Used CSS hover transitions and opacity-based background shifts to provide immediate visual feedback to the merchant when navigating the table.

2. **Clean Data Mapping**:
   The table now maps internal database IDs to "Human-Readable" rows, ensuring the merchant always has the "Database Reference" if they need support.

3. **Safe Deletion Flow**:
   Integrated JavaScript `confirm()` dialogs directly into the form submission to provide a final safety check before data is permanently removed.

## How to use:
Merchants use this page as their primary hub for managing what is visible to the public. If a product needs a price update or needs to be removed, it happens here.
