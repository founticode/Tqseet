# TQSEET Platform: Merchant Onboarding & Financial Logic (Phase 11 Summary)

This document outlines the professional architectural changes made to transform TQSEET from a simple shop into a secure, multi-merchant BNPL marketplace.

## 1. Merchant Onboarding Gate (Security First)
We implemented a dual-track registration system. 
- Merchants register separately and are placed in a 'pending' state.
- **Security Check**: An automated "Product Gate" was added. Unapproved merchants are physically blocked from listing products or accessing sales tools. 
- **Admin Audit**: Admins must manually review the "Business Profile" (Store Name/Description) before a merchant can go live.

## 2. Professional Financial Logic (The "Klarna Model")
We moved away from hardcoded numbers to a dynamic, agreement-based financial engine.

### A. Dynamic Commission Structure
- **Admin-Led Pricing**: The Admin now sets the commission rate (%) individually for each merchant during the approval process.
- **Data Integrity**: This rate is saved in the `merchants` table and is used to calculate the platform's cut for every single order.
- **Merchant Transparency**: Approved merchants see their "Partner Agreement" fee clearly on their dashboard to ensure financial trust.

### B. Gross vs. Net Earnings
- The system now calculates:
  1. `total_price`: What the customer pays.
  2. `commission`: What the platform keeps.
  3. `merchant_earning`: What the platform owes the merchant.

## 3. Advanced Admin Monitoring
We built two major "Command Centers" for platform oversight:

### A. The System Analytics Dashboard
- Provides a high-level view of **GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)** and **Net Platform Revenue**.
- Features a **Cashflow Health** monitor that calculates "Collected" vs. "Outstanding" debt.
- Tracks **Merchant Performance** (who is selling the most).

### B. The Installment Wall
- A master ledger of every single installment in the system.
- Includes **Automated Overdue Detection**: If a payment date passes and status is 'unpaid', the system triggers a Red alert for the Admin.

## 4. Enhanced User Experience (UX)
- **Active vs. History**: The "My Shopping" page now intelligently splits into "Active Plans" (ongoing debt) and "Purchase History" (fully owned items).
- **Ownership Verification**: Fully paid items get a "🛡️ OWNED" badge, providing the user with a psychological sense of completion and security.
