# Professional Credit Scoring & Verification (Phase 12 Upgrade)

We upgraded the credit engine from a "trust-based" system to a "verification-based" system, mirroring the standards of Klarna and Tabby.

## What we built:
A secure intake and auditing pipeline for user financial data.

## Why we built it:
A BNPL platform is essentially a lender. Lending money based on unverified text is high-risk. By requiring physical proof of income and automating the limit calculation, we reduce platform risk and increase professional credibility.

## Key Technical Features:

1. **Document Intake Pipeline**:
   Users must upload a PDF or image of their salary slip. The system handles the file storage and links it to their unique financial profile.

2. **Automated Credit Algorithm**:
   Implemented a `1.5x` salary multiplier logic. 
   - *Logic*: If user earns `X`, their buying power is `1.5 * X`.
   - This removes human bias from the credit assignment process.

3. **Admin Audit Center**:
   The Admin UI was upgraded to act as a document viewer. Admins can cross-reference the uploaded paper against the user's claims before granting approval.

## Business Rule:
The Admin does not set the limit. The Admin verifies the **truth** of the data. The **system** sets the limit.
