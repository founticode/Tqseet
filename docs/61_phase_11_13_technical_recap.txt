# TQSEET Technical Recap: Phases 11, 12 & 13

This recap details the transformation of TQSEET into a complete, secure, and user-centric BNPL platform.

## 1. Merchant Ecosystem & Revenue (Phase 11)
- **Multi-Merchant Core**: Enabled unique commission rates per store.
- **Revenue Logic**: Automated calculation of platform earnings vs. merchant payouts.
- **Admin Reconciliation**: Built reports to track exactly how much the platform makes per order.

## 2. Professional KYC Gate (Phase 12)
- **Hardened Verification**: Introduced mandatory CIN (Identity) and Salary Proof (Financial) uploads.
- **Automated Scoring**: Replaced manual credit limits with a "1.5x Salary" automated logic.
- **Buying Guard**: Implemented a real-time lock on the checkout process. Users cannot shop until both Identity and Financials are approved.

## 3. Profile & Account Management (Phase 13)
- **Unified Identity**: Created the "Real Profile" page with custom Avatar support.
- **Universal Settings**: Built specialized management pages for all roles:
    - User Settings: Personal & KYC data.
    - Merchant Settings: Store branding & Partnership status.
    - Admin Settings: Platform credentials.
- **Security Engine**: Implemented a role-aware password update system with current-password verification.
